What changes everywhere

  • The 1,180 px cap goes: the screen fills the window beside the 220 px sidebar, up to a 2,400 px safety cap.
  • Column counts come from content (auto-fit with a minimum card width, container queries on the card), not from fixed splits or window breakpoints.
  • Type and card padding never scale; wider windows get more columns, not bigger text. The gutter steps from 24 to 32 px and the gap from 16 to 20 px at 1,920+.
  • Every text slot declares wrap, clamp (with a way to open it) or truncate (with the full text reachable).
