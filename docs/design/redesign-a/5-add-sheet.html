The “+” sheet: current vs proposed

The “+” sheet stops being five forms. It becomes three one-tap paths (the usual, pot shares, the next set) and Tell Claude for everything else.

Five tabs: Food, Workout, Set, Reading, Tell Claude (last). Each is a form: For chips, a Day field, a Meal select, then search → pick → amount → how measured → Save.No tabs. One short list, in order: your usual (one tap each, with Undo), today's pots (your share in one tap), sets (only on a training day: the next set as prescribed), and Tell Claude for anything else.Quick entry only for the usual, pot shares and sets against the prescription (decided).
