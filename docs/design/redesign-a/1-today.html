Today: current vs proposed

Today opens with the week, then one line for what waits on you, then the day itself. Every form on Today becomes a mark, a one-tap usual or a question.

Opens on the date bar, then the needs-you strip: at most 3 cards of mixed kinds, “+3 more”.Your week first (digest spec §6.2: dates, the Next-week items, Read the week, Dismiss), then one line for everything waiting, which opens Waiting on you.Today speaks first, and the drafts stop being split between the strip, the tiers and the pot card.
A draft about you carries its own “Yes, confirm · Not mine…” bar inside the meal tier, and the pot card has its own ask.The draft stays visible in its tier, marked with a haldi rule and “Logged by Kabir · waiting for you in Waiting”. The pot card shows your share as “waiting for you”. Confirming happens in one list.One hand-off object, one place to act on it (decided).
“Add to …” under every tier opens the full food form (search, pick, amount, how measured, Save: about 12–15 taps for the usual breakfast).An empty meal offers the usual for that slot in one tap, or “Something else: Tell Claude”. No per-tier form.Quick entry stays only for the usual, pot shares and sets.
