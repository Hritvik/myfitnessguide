Desktop layout: how should the wide screen be used?

Same app, same data, a second layout above ~1024 px. Shown on Today for Asha, with the invented sample household. All three put navigation in a sidebar (your earlier choice). Pick the one that feels right, or tell me what to mix.

A

One wide column

The phone screen, wider: the macro strip and pot get room, tables show inline. Simplest; least new design. Wastes width on Today.

B

The counter: your day beside the household

Two columns on every tab: your view on the left, the household (or the thing you'd check next) on the right. Today + Household; Train next session + history; Progress chart + its table.

C

List and detail

A list of days, sessions or catalogue items on the left; the open one on the right. Best for catching up and editing; less "at a glance" for today.
