Food proposals and answered questions in scheduled Inbox resolution

Mockups for docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-09-food-proposals-design.md. They build on redesign A's Waiting on you (docs/design/redesign-a/6-waiting.html) and use its tokens, cards and frames. Phone frames are 390 px; wide frames 1,440 px.

Theme

In one line: a scheduled run asks the note's sender instead of skipping the note, turns an unknown food into a proposal instead of a guess, and the household's catalogue keeper approves it on an editable New food card. Then the run drafts the food, and the drafts wait in the member's Waiting as before.

The screens

Question cardThe member's “Claude asks” card for an unknown food: answer chips, “Something else…”, and Add a label photo. Photo uploadPreparing the photo on the phone, the confirmation after sending (“Asha will see it when approving the food”), and Tell Claude with a label. New foodThe keeper's editable card: the closest existing foods first, values with provenance chips, “values needed”, the label photo, a reason she writes; phone 390 and wide 1,440. New recipeIngredient first, recipe after: the wrap is approved, then the next run proposes the recipe with every line in the foods. Rejected noteThe keeper's reason reaching the member as a neutral note, and the run's follow-up question.

One note, start to finish

  1. Noor tells Claude“a bowl of the new muesli with milk, and black coffee”, from the “+” sheet.
  2. The scheduled run asksThe muesli isn't in the catalogue, so it asks Noor, the sender, before writing anything. Her note now waits on a question.
  3. Noor answers“A packet from the shop”, with a label photo, resized and stripped on her phone. The answer comes back as an Inbox entry.
  4. The next run proposesIt reads the photo and proposes the food with each value's source; the rows the photo cut off are “values needed”. It writes nothing to the catalogue.
  5. Asha approvesOn her New food card she checks it isn't one of the closest existing foods, fills in fat, writes why, and approves. The real upsert_food_source runs as her. The photo is no longer shown to her.
  6. The next run draftsNoor's breakfast is drafted against the new food, and the note is closed. The drafts wait in Noor's Waiting for her to confirm, as any Inbox draft does.

Settled

  • A proposal is the upsert input once complete: only energy, protein, carbs, fat and the reference amount may be missing, each marked “values needed”. Approve stays disabled until the keeper fills them, and the server refuses an approval with any still empty.
  • Ingredient first, recipe after; photos built now, as their own release; a card on the Cloudflare account is fine if R2 asks (Hritvik, 9 Oct).
  • This design amends redesign A in six named ways (spec, “Amends redesign A”), approved with it.
